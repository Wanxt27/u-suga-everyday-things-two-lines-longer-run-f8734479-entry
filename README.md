A make.fun entry for [Everyday Things, Two Lines, Longer Run](https://make.fun/bounty/u-suga-everyday-things-two-lines-longer-run-f8734479).

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only) writes exactly two lines — 321 characters — to `/output/entry.txt`:

> A toaster is a tiny sunbed where a slice of bread lies under glowing wires until it turns golden brown, then pops up when its timer says so.
> The sunbed fits because heat browns bread the way sun browns skin, but it leaves out that the heat comes from electric wires, not the sun, and the bread never chooses when to stop.

Line 1 explains a toaster to a 10-year-old with a single analogy a child can picture: bread sunbathing under glowing wires until it turns golden, then popping up on its timer. Line 2 is honest about the mapping: heat browning is what the sunbed gets right, while the heat source (electric wires, not the sun) and the bread's lack of choice are what it leaves out.

Run it with:

```sh
node main.ts
```
